A forecast instrument

It shows you what it doesn't know.

Most weather apps fill every cell. When a reading is missing they put a zero in it — a UV index of 0.0, a visibility of 10 km, a sunrise at solar noon, a sub-zero low on a day forecast to stay above freezing. Nimbus renders absence as an em dash, and the instrument tells you which is which.
